Momentum & Collisions Lab

Crash carts on a track with chosen masses and velocities. Compare elastic, inelastic and perfectly inelastic collisions with momentum and kinetic energy bar charts, read impulse from a force-time graph for a rigid wall and a crumple zone, watch the same collision from the center-of-mass frame, and try a two-dimensional glancing collision of air hockey pucks.
