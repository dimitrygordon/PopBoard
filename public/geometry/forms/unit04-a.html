Show equations, proof reasons and ordered correspondence. All figures are supplied inline. Do not use trig or the distance formula for SSA explanations.

Original aligned items. Related scope: Jacobs 4.2 to 4.7, pp. 139 to 175; Chapter 4 review, pp. 176 to 180.

1.

Triangle TUV ≅ triangle HJK. Which side matches UV, and which angle matches angle T?

2.

Triangle LMN ≅ triangle CBA, LM = 7a - 6, and CB = 57. Find a and LM.

3.

HI = RS = 8, HJ = RT = 13, and angle IHJ = angle SRT = 37 degrees. Prove triangle HIJ ≅ triangle RST. Then show IJ = ST.

4.

Triangles UVW and DFE have angle V = angle F = 48 degrees, angle W = angle E = 73 degrees, and VW = FE = 12. Prove the triangles are congruent. Which side matches UW?

5.

Segments JK and LN meet at O, the midpoint of both. JO = 4 and LO = 9. Prove triangle JOL ≅ triangle KON. Then give KO and NO.

6.

Triangle FGH ≅ triangle UTS. Angle G = (3c + 8) degrees and angle T = 50 degrees. Find c and give the reason for your equation.

7.

In triangle PQR, PQ = PR, angle Q = (2e + 14) degrees, and angle R = (5e - 16) degrees. Find e and each base angle.

8.

Triangle VXW has angle X = angle W, VX = 3g + 4, and VW = g + 32. Prove the legs are equal without using the converse theorem. Then find g and the leg length.

9.

Triangles ABC and UVW have matching angles of 42, 58 and 80 degrees, in that order. AB = 6 and UV = 15. Explain why AAA can't prove they are congruent, even if you match the corners a different way.

10.

In convex quadrilateral KLMN, KL = KN and LM = NM. A proof starts with 'angle LKM = angle NKM by CPCTC.' Fix the proof so it shows KM bisects angle LKN.

11.

E and F are 14 units apart. Circles of radius 10 centered at E and F cross at G and H, on opposite sides of EF. Prove triangle EFG ≅ triangle EFH. Would radius 7 give two crossing points for the perpendicular bisector construction?

12.

Use the figure. Q1 and Q2 are on the same ray from O. OP = 10, OQ1 = 2 and OQ2 = 10. Both Q points lie on one circle centered at P. Prove that triangles OPQ1 and OPQ2 have matching SSA parts but are not congruent. Don't use trig or the distance formula.
