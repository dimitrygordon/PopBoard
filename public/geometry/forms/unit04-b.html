Show equations, proof reasons and ordered correspondence. All figures are supplied inline. Do not use trig or the distance formula for SSA explanations.

Original aligned items. Related scope: Jacobs 4.2 to 4.7, pp. 139 to 175; Chapter 4 review, pp. 176 to 180.

1.

Triangle WXY ≅ triangle QPR. Which side matches WY, and which angle matches angle X?

2.

Triangle EFG ≅ triangle RQP, EG = 6b + 5, and RP = 71. Find b and EG.

3.

KL = VW = 9, KM = VZ = 14, and angle LKM = angle WVZ = 43 degrees. Prove triangle KLM ≅ triangle VWZ. Then show LM = WZ.

4.

Triangles NOP and LSR have angle O = angle S = 56 degrees, angle P = angle R = 64 degrees, and OP = SR = 15. Prove the triangles are congruent. Which side matches NP?

5.

Segments AC and BD meet at Z, the midpoint of both. AZ = 7 and BZ = 12. Prove triangle AZB ≅ triangle CZD. Then give CZ and DZ.

6.

Triangle JMN ≅ triangle VUW. Angle N = (5d - 9) degrees and angle W = 76 degrees. Find d and give the reason for your equation.

7.

In triangle STU, ST = SU, angle T = (4f - 11) degrees, and angle U = (2f + 15) degrees. Find f and each base angle.

8.

Triangle HIK has angle I = angle K, HI = 5h - 8, and HK = 2h + 43. Prove the legs are equal without using the converse theorem. Then find h and the leg length.

9.

Triangles DEF and LMN have matching angles of 36, 63 and 81 degrees, in that order. DE = 8 and LM = 20. Explain why AAA can't prove they are congruent, even if you match the corners a different way.

10.

In convex quadrilateral RSTU, RS = RU and ST = UT. A proof starts with 'angle SRT = angle URT by CPCTC.' Fix the proof so it shows RT bisects angle SRU.

11.

G and H are 18 units apart. Circles of radius 12 centered at G and H cross at J and K, on opposite sides of GH. Prove triangle GHJ ≅ triangle GHK. Would radius 9 give two crossing points for the perpendicular bisector construction?

12.

Use the figure. N1 and N2 are on the same ray from L. LM = 17, LN1 = 5 and LN2 = 11. Both N points lie on one circle centered at M. Prove that triangles LMN1 and LMN2 have matching SSA parts but are not congruent. Don't use trig or the distance formula.
