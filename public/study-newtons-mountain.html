Newton's Mountain VR

Stand on an imaginary mountain next to a cannon, fire level shots at faster and faster speeds, and find the speed where the ball stops landing. Then work out why it is still falling. Works on a screen, on a phone you move around, and on a Meta Quest 3 in VR or passthrough AR.
