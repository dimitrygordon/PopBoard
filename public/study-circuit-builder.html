Circuit Builder

About this board

Drag a part to move it, and drag it off the board to remove it. Drag from one lead (the small ring at either end of a part) to another to add a wire, and tap a wire to remove it. Tap a switch to flip it, and tap any part to read its current and voltage.

Put an ammeter in the loop, in series. Put a voltmeter across a part, in parallel: the Clip voltmeter button does this for the part you have selected.
