# Newton's Mountain VR credits

- Stars: HYG database v4.4 (astronexus, David Nash), CC BY-SA 4.0, via the Night Sky star layer (`public/night-sky/data`).
- Earth and Moon maps: Solar System Scope textures, CC BY 4.0, inside `public/gravity-orbits/models/earth_2k.glb` and `moon_2k.glb`.
- ISS model (shown only with the ISS ring): NASA media, `public/gravity-orbits/models/iss.glb`.
- Air density: U.S. Standard Atmosphere, 1976, NASA TM X-74335 (U.S. Government work), `ussa76-density-5km.json` (every 5 km, 0 to 1,000 km).
- Earth constants: the lesson pair from `public/newtons-cannonball-fire/ncf-config.js`.
- three.js r170 (MIT). Font: Inter (SIL OFL 1.1).
- The cannon, platform, mountain, trails and panel are drawn by code.
