Ripple Tank

A top-down view of shallow water. Tap to add dippers that make circular waves, drag them around, splash the surface, add walls with one or two slits, and change the frequency to see interference and diffraction.

About this tank

Tap the water to add a dipper, drag a dipper to move it, and drag it out of the tank to remove it. In Sandbox you can also splash the water or drop blocks.

The grid gets coarser by itself if your device slows down, so the waves keep moving smoothly.
