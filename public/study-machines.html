Ideal machines conserve energy, so W_in = W_out and a smaller force travels farther. Friction, rope mass, and pulley mass are the real machine: useful work stays mgh (or F_load times the load's distance) and efficiency = useful output / input × 100%. g = 9.80665 m/s². W = Fd for a constant force parallel to the displacement.
