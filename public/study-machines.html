    Work = force × distance

    Ideal machines conserve energy, so W_in = W_out and a smaller force travels farther. Friction, rope mass, pulley mass, bearing torque, and wedge deformation are the real machine: useful work stays F_load times the load's distance and efficiency = useful output / input × 100%. A compound machine multiplies the stage MAs and multiplies the stage efficiencies. g = 9.80665 m/s². W = Fd for a constant force parallel to the displacement.
