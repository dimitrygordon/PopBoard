# Newton's Cannonball Fire

A Physics 101 Module 7 Lesson 2 sandbox: Newton's mountain-cannon thought experiment, plus a step-by-step derivation of orbital speed and period and worked practice problems.

- Page: `/study-newtons-cannonball-fire.html`
- Earth constants come from Orbit Lab (`GM_E = 3.98e14` m³/s², `R_E = 6.38e6` m) and the flights reuse `orbit-lab/ol-model.js`. Do not invent replacements.
- Student wording lives in `ncf-strings.js` and is a Socrates placeholder until `PLACEHOLDER` is flipped to false.
- Numbers are computed in `ncf-physics.js` and marked pending Tesla (`TESLA_PENDING`).
- Art is isolated in `ncf-art.js`. See `art/README.md`.
