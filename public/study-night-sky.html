Night Sky VR

A sky simulator for any date, time and place from 1900 to 2050. Drag to look around, tap a star for its name, magnitude, colour, distance and position, and turn constellation lines, art and names on or off. On a Meta Quest 3, press Enter VR to stand under the sky in the headset.
