Beyond 3D

A picture book about lower and higher dimensions: from a point to a line, a square and a cube, a flatlander who meets a ball, shadows and slices of a cube, counting corners and edges, building a tesseract, its shadow in 3D with 3D and 4D rotation, slices of a tesseract and a hypersphere, unfolding a cube and a tesseract into nets, and a sandbox.
