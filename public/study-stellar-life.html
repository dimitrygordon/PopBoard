Stellar Life Cycle VR

Follow five stars from birth to death: 0.2, 1, 3, 15 and 40 times the mass of the Sun. Watch a cloud collapse into a star and planets, follow each star across a live H-R diagram from real stellar models, and see it end as a white dwarf, a neutron star or a black hole. Predict before each stage plays. On a Meta Quest 3, press Enter VR to watch in the headset.

Sources and credits

Star tracks: MIST v1.2 stellar models (Choi et al. 2016; Dotter 2016), solar metallicity, rotating models (v/vcrit = 0.4), interpolated by equivalent evolutionary point. The 1 Sun track is adjusted slightly so today's Sun matches measured values.

White dwarf cooling: Bédard, Bergeron, Brassard and Fontaine 2020, thick hydrogen models, courtesy of the Montreal white dwarf group (astro.umontreal.ca/~bergeron/CoolingModels).

Late fuel stage times: Woosley, Heger and Weaver 2002. Dashed lines are drawn, not modelled: 0.2 Suns after its model ends (predicted) and 40 Suns after about 5 million years (models differ from here).
