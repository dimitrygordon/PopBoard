Problem 21: How much mass stretches the spring to 12.0 cm?

A spring's length follows y = (0.15 cm/g) x + 8.2 cm, where x is the mass in grams hung from it.

What mass makes the spring 12.0 cm long?

Step 1: Picture the line

PLACEHOLDER (Socrates will write this step). The slope 0.15 cm/g is the stretch per gram; 8.2 cm is the length with no mass. Each dot is one mass reading.

Step 2: Find 12.0 cm on the line

PLACEHOLDER (Socrates will write this step). Go across from 12.0 cm to the line, then down to the mass axis. The red arrow lands near 25 g; now confirm it with algebra.

Step 3: Solve for x

PLACEHOLDER (Socrates will write this step). Subtract the intercept, then divide by the slope.

Try it: Drag the mass

mass x = 25 g
length y = 12.0 cm
stretch = 3.8 cm

Drag the block, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Both arrows grow with the mass.

Answer

About 25 g.

Check: at 25 g the stretch is 0.15 × 25 = 3.75 cm, and 8.2 + 3.8 = 12.0 cm.
